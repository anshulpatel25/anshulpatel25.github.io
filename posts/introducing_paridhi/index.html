Introducing Paridhi: Discovering Authentic Local Gems Beyond Delivery App Markups with MCP

October 17, 2026 7-minute read
Ai • Mcp • Model-Context-Protocol • Gemini • Cloudflare • Python • Agentic • Open-Source

Have you ever opened a food delivery or discovery app to order lunch, only to notice that the exact same roll, burger, or thali costs 20% to 30% more on the app than if you walked into the restaurant? Layer on delivery fees, platform convenience fees, and surge charges, and a quick meal suddenly comes with a steep aggregator premium. 🍔💸

Even worse is what happens to discovery. Searching for “best coffee” or “top street food” rarely shows you the most authentic local spots anymore. Instead, your feed is dominated by whoever paid the highest ad bid or signed the most exclusive merchant deal. Meanwhile, the true hidden gems—the 30-year-old family bakery, the corner tea stall with a 4.7-star rating, or the incredible local bistro right down the street from your office—remain completely invisible because they don’t play the aggregator advertising game.

What if your AI assistant could act as your personal, unbiased local scout? An agent that understands real-world geography, searches within your exact walking or driving perimeter, and discovers authentic local spots with verified reviews without sponsored noise or marked-up menus?

Meet Paridhi (परिधि) — an open-source Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that equips modern AI agents with spatial awareness to discover, filter, and enrich local points of interest. 🚀📍

🧐 The Problem: Why Commercial Discovery Apps Let Us Down Link to heading

For years, we relied on delivery and discovery apps because they solved an undeniable logistics challenge: bringing menus and storefronts to our phones. However, over time, the economics of these platforms shifted in ways that hurt both consumers and neighborhood businesses:

  1. The 20%–30% “Menu Markup”: To offset high commission rates charged by aggregators, restaurants routinely inflate their listed prices. When you add taxes, handling, and delivery charges, you are paying significantly more for the convenience of ordering through an intermediary.
  2. Sponsored Clutter & Algorithmic Bias: Commercial platforms prioritize merchants who purchase sponsored placement or buy into premium promotion tiers. The first ten recommendations you see are often paid ads rather than the highest-quality local options.
  3. The Hidden Gem Blind Spot: Many of the best food joints, artisan roasters, and cultural landmarks near our homes or workspaces do not offer app delivery or maintain marketing budgets. They thrive purely on word of mouth and genuine local reputation, making them invisible on commercial delivery platforms.
  4. Rigid Geofences: Delivery apps restrict what you can see based on delivery fleet coverage. If a great spot is just 200 meters outside an arbitrary delivery zone, you never even learn that it exists.

When you are sitting at your desk or walking around a neighborhood, you don’t need a sponsored advertising feed. You need unbiased, spatially accurate local intelligence.

🌐 What is Paridhi? Link to heading

In Sanskrit, the word Paridhi (परिधि) translates to perimeter, circumference, or boundary. That exact concept forms the foundation of this project.

Paridhi connects your AI agents (such as Gemini, Claude, Antigravity, or Cursor) directly to authoritative geospatial data using the Model Context Protocol. Rather than presenting algorithmic rankings influenced by ad dollars, Paridhi operates strictly within a defined spatial circle around your current location or a specified neighborhood.

Key Technical Highlights Link to heading

  • 🎯 Strict Spatial Perimeters: Applies haversine distance filtering to ensure every discovered point of interest strictly falls within your chosen radius.
  • 🛡️ Predictable Clamping: Automatically bounds search radii to [100m, 50,000m], preventing runaway queries or accidental massive geofences.
  • 📐 Hexagonal / Clean Architecture: Built with a Ports & Adapters design that cleanly separates the domain logic from external providers. It supports Google Maps Platform out of the box, with built-in mock providers for fast, offline unit testing.
  • 💰 Billing Optimization via Field Masks: Uses explicit Places API (New) field masks (X-Goog-FieldMask) to request only necessary attributes, keeping API costs minimal.
  • 🔗 Agent-Ready Markdown Output: Automatically formats discovered places into rich Markdown tables with deep links to Google Maps pins and verified ratings.

Why Cloudflare MCP Gateway? Link to heading

In this setup, API traffic between the agentic harness and Gemini routes through via Anshul Patel Gateway which is actually Cloudflare MCP Gateway. This architecture brings several enterprise-grade benefits to everyday agent workflows:

  1. Authentication & Authorization: Cloudflare MCP Gateway acts as a secure reverse proxy, allowing you to centralize API credential management and apply zero-trust access policies without baking sensitive secrets into client runtimes.
  2. Caching & Lower Latency: Repeated spatial lookups or intermediate agent reasoning steps can be cached at the Cloudflare MCP Gateway edge, reducing both latency and token costs.
  3. Rate Limiting & Cost Guardrails: Autonomous agents can occasionally enter unintended retry loops. The MCP Gateway enforces hard rate limits and budget thresholds to protect against surprise API bills.
  4. Unified Observability: Every request, prompt token, completion token, and latency metric is logged in a centralized dashboard, giving you complete visibility into how your agent interacts with tools.
